One Window. Two Worlds. One Way Home.

Artemis II is a voyage designed to carry a crew beyond low Earth orbit and around the Moon.
From Orion's window, Earth will no longer fill the sky. It will become a brilliant, fragile sphere suspended in darkness.

Imagine the unforgettable frame: the blue Earth and the ancient Moon together,
separated by vast silence but joined by one gaze.
