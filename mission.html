A Story Drawn Across Space

  1. 01 — Launch: Leaving the Blue

    Orion begins with fire, acceleration, and a horizon that quickly curves. The first great change is simple: Earth becomes visible as a whole.

  2. 02 — Outbound: The Long Look Back

    Distance changes scale. The crew's home becomes a small colored world in a field without edges, sharpening the meaning of every return trajectory.

  3. 03 — Lunar Flyby: Meeting the Far Side

    The Moon's unseen hemisphere enters view: old impact basins, fierce light, and long shadows. Orion bends around this companion world.

  4. 04 — Homeward: Carrying the View Back

    The path turns toward Earth. The pictures, observations, and human memories of deep space travel inward with the spacecraft.
